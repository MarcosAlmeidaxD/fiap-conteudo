## TL;DR
This is the project brief for building and pitching a sales prediction model for the "Melhores Compras" e-commerce challenge.

## What's Inside
- **Dataset Schema (`sales.csv`):** Includes Date, Weekly_Sales, Holiday_Flag, Temp, Fuel_Price (divide by 1000), CPI, and Unemployment (divide by 1000).
- **Modeling Challenge:** Build a predictive algorithm for weekly sales and justify your choice by analyzing which variables (like holidays or fuel prices) actually move the needle.
- **The Pitch:** Create a non-technical presentation or dashboard to explain results to a board that doesn't know what "accuracy" or "CPI" means.
- **Evaluation Criteria:** Code is only half the battle—your grade depends heavily on your ability to argue your technical decisions and communicate value.

## Worth Knowing
The board cares more about *why* you chose the model and *how* you explain it than the raw metrics; dropping a jargon-heavy score like "accuracy 0.26" without context is a guaranteed way to fail the presentation.
